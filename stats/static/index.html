How points work

Each chapter you play earns points: 1 per common infected, 5 per special, 15 per witch, 10 for finishing a tank plus 1 per 50 damage dealt to tanks, 10 per teammate healed, 8 per revive, 12 per defib and 6 per closet rescue. Making it to the saferoom adds 25, escaping a finale 75 more. Friendly fire costs 1 per 2 damage, going down 5 and dying 20.

The total is then multiplied by the difficulty: Easy ×0.5, Normal ×1, Advanced ×1.5, Expert ×2. If the difficulty changes during a chapter, the easiest setting counts.

Ranked leaves out "boosted" chapters: ones played with cheats, infinite ammo, a tank run, double loot, or an admin handing out items, health or extra infected. Points per hour need at least an hour played. Bots aren't counted, and neither is what a bot does while you're idle.
